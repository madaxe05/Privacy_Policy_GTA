## Information we collect

The App does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The following data is stored **locally on your device only** and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

- Favorited cheats
- Recently viewed cheats (last 10)
- Platform preference (PlayStation / Xbox / PC)
- App settings (dark mode, haptic feedback, notification preferences)

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes it. We have no access to it and no copy of it exists on any server we control.
