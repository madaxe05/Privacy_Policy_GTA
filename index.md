# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026
**App name:** GTA 6 Guide & GTA 5 Cheats (Android package com.sohan.cheatcodegta)
**Developer:** Veritas Studios
**Contact:** xero.by.suman@gmail.com

This app is an independent, fan-made reference tool for GTA cheat codes and GTA 5 / GTA 6 guides. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Game titles are the property of their owners and are used only to describe what the app covers.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, who receives it, and what choices you have.

## The short version

- No account and no sign-in.
- We only ask for your name and email address if you choose to enter a community giveaway.
- Your favorites and settings stay on your device.
- The app uses Google services (Firebase and AdMob) that collect some technical and usage data automatically. Details below.
- Ads are shown through Google AdMob and its mediation partners. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later.
- Optional Premium purchases are paid through Google Play and checked through RevenueCat.
- We do not sell your data.

## Data that stays on your device

Stored only on your device and never sent to us:

- Favorited cheats and saved guides
- Recently viewed cheats
- Your selected game and platform
- Settings (sound, haptics, notification preferences)
- Counters the app uses to space out ads
- The list of days you opened the app (used for giveaway activity rules; only a count is ever sent, and only if you enter a giveaway)

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Community giveaways (optional)

If you choose to enter a giveaway, the app sends your **name, email address, country, chosen prize, the confirmations you ticked, the number of days you opened the app during the activity period, the app version and the time of entry** to our Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) database.

- We use it only to run that giveaway, check the winner's eligibility and contact the winner. See the [official giveaway rules](giveaway-rules).
- Entries are never shown to other users, shared for marketing or sold.
- Each entry is stored under a code made from the campaign and your email address. Anyone who already knows an email address could check whether it entered a giveaway, but could never see the entry itself.
- Entry data is deleted within 90 days of the draw, except the winner's record, which is kept as long as needed to deliver the prize and meet legal duties.
- Email us to have your entry deleted at any time.

## Premium purchases (optional)

If you buy Premium, payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. We use **RevenueCat** to check whether your purchase is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created on your device, your Google Play purchase records for this app (product, purchase token, dates, price and currency), and basic device and app information. It is used only to provide and restore Premium. RevenueCat's policy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>

## Data collected automatically by Google services

The app uses these services. They run on Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address when your device contacts them.

**Firebase Analytics.** Helps us understand which parts of the app are used. It records events such as opening or sharing a guide, opening a notification, and ad performance events. If you use the Guides search, the text you search for is recorded as an event. Analytics also uses a pseudonymous app-instance identifier and basic device information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address).

**Firebase Crashlytics.** If the app crashes, a report is sent containing the crash details, app version, device model and operating system version, so we can fix the bug.

**Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications).** If you allow notifications, the app registers with Google to receive them. This involves a push token and an installation identifier. Your device is subscribed to notification topics that match the categories you switch on in Settings. You can turn notifications off in the app's Settings or in your device's system settings.

**Firebase Remote Config.** Fetches app settings (for example how often ads appear) so they can change without an app update.

**Cloud Firestore.** Downloads the guide content and giveaway details shown in the app (read-only, no login), and stores giveaway entries if you choose to enter.

Google and these partners may collect device identifiers such as your advertising ID, your IP address, device information and information about the ads you see and interact with, to deliver, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Their own privacy policies apply:

- Google: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>
- AppLovin: <https://www.applovin.com/privacy/>
- Unity: <https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>
- Meta: <https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/>

- **Consent.** In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in some US states, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. Where that form applies to you, a **Privacy Options** entry appears in the app's Settings so you can review or change your choice at any time.
- **Ad content.** The app asks for ads up to a Teen content rating.
- **Device controls.** You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings.

## Other network requests

- **Fonts.** The app's font files are bundled; older versions loaded them from Google Fonts.
- **Guide images.** Some guide images are loaded from third-party websites, which can see your IP address and device details when the image is requested.
- **Update check.** The app downloads a small file hosted on GitHub Pages to see whether a new version is available. GitHub can see your IP address.
- **Links.** If you tap a link, it opens in another app or your browser, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

## Legal bases and your rights (EEA, UK and similar regions)

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, we process analytics and crash data based on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it, advertising data based on your consent where consent is required, giveaway entries to run the giveaway you chose to enter, and purchase data to provide the purchase you made. You can withdraw consent at any time through **Privacy Options** in Settings. You may ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of data we hold about you, and you may object to processing or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## Retention

Analytics, crash and messaging data are kept by Google under the retention settings of the Firebase project, then deleted or aggregated. Giveaway entries are deleted within 90 days of the draw (winner records as described above). Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as needed to provide Premium and for legal and accounting duties. Data on your device is kept until you clear it or uninstall the app.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed by the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob and its mediation partners, Google Play, RevenueCat, GitHub Pages and third-party image hosts for the requests described). These providers may process data in countries outside your own.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Giveaways are open only to people of the minimum age stated in each campaign (at least 18). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
